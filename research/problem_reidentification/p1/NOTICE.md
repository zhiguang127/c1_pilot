# PMData attribution

The frozen P1 measurement windows are derived from **PMData: A Sports Logging Dataset** by Vajira Thambawita and colleagues (MMSys 2020), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/3339825.3394926.

Original dataset: https://datasets.simula.no/pmdata/ ; original repository: https://osf.io/vx4bk/ .

PMData's original license is **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/ . The derived measurement data retain that license. No commercial permission is inferred from a third-party mirror.

Changes: a prespecified subset of Fitbit files was imported; records were projected to selected fields; qualifying duplicate sleep exports were collapsed; missingness was preserved; dates were replaced with relative days and weekdays; participant identifiers were replaced with keyed pseudonyms; fixed 42-day windows were formed. No measurements or labels were synthesized for this release. No claim is made that pseudonymization prevents re-identification of publicly released longitudinal records.

Knowledge items are existing independently written short paraphrases with original source attribution in each record. They are not PMData annotations and their sources do not supply recommendation-priority labels.
